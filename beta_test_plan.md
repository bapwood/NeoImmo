## **1. Project context**
Neoimmo is an innovative platform that makes real estate investment accessible to everyone, even those on a small budget. Thanks to tokenization, each user can purchase shares in real estate properties for just a few dozen euros, making it easy to enter a market traditionally reserved for large capital investments. The income generated by these properties is automatically converted and paid out in cryptocurrency, offering the possibility of receiving 100% of the returns without immediate taxation. These gains can then be automatically reinvested, allowing capital to grow exponentially thanks to the mechanism of compound interest.

## **2. User Roles**
The following roles will be involved in beta testing.

| **Role Name**  | **Description** |
|--------|----------------------|
| Admin        | An administrator manage organisation settings, create and delete properties, assign roles, and control access to resources |
| Employee     | A regular user who can invest in properties, sell his parts of a property, save a property, pre-register for properties, manage his account and connect his crypto-wallet |
